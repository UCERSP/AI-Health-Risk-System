YOUR HEALTH, YOUR INSIGHTS

Understand Your Health Assessment

HealthAI is an educational project that uses the health information you enter to generate an estimated result. The result may help you understand the information provided by the system.

1. Enter Your Details

Provide your age, height, weight, blood pressure and blood glucose information.

2. Analyze Information

The system processes the submitted values using its configured health-risk model.

3. View Your Result

Review the estimated result, measurement graphs and available precautionary suggestions.

4. Track Your History

Signed-in users can view their own saved assessments. Guest assessments are not saved to account history.

Important: This project is an educational prototype, not a medical diagnostic tool. Its results should not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

AI-Based Health Risk Analysis

This AI Health Summer Training Project has been developed by Shailja Pandey, a student of the 7th Semester at United College of Research and Science, PRAYAGRAJ. The project was undertaken as part of the summer training program organised by the Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT), Prayagraj. It aims to apply artificial intelligence concepts and technologies to the healthcare domain as part of the student's academic and practical learning experience.
